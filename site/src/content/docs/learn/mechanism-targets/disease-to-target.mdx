## Foundations

A target hypothesis should connect disease context, molecular mechanism,
perturbation, and an intervention that can be tested. The
[DepMap portal](https://depmap.org/portal/), [Reactome](https://reactome.org/),
and [BioGRID](https://thebiogrid.org/) provide different evidence types; no one
source makes a causal claim complete.

## Practical workflow

Specify the target decision unit: a gene, a protein complex, a pathway node, or
a biomarker-defined subgroup. Keep the sample provenance, perturbation design,
and endpoint attached. Use a traceable evidence table before applying any
ranking model.

## Evaluation

Look for cohort, donor, lineage, and temporal leakage. Correlated dependency
and expression signals can agree while still failing to predict intervention
response. The cheapest falsifier is an orthogonal perturbation or assay rather
than another model score.

## Frontier and open questions

Biomedical language models such as [BioGPT](https://github.com/microsoft/BioGPT)
may help retrieve hypotheses, but retrieval quality cannot turn unsupported
text into causal evidence.

## A bounded worked decision and independent task

Read [biological representations](/learn/representations/biological-representations/) before defining a target claim. In a constructed locus, a disease association sits near genes T and U. A perturbation of T changes a phenotype in one cell context, but no bridge from the associated variant to T is supplied. The regional association and the perturbation therefore answer different questions; combining their names does not establish that inhibiting T benefits patients.

Write two competing explanations and a discriminating observation. Which unresolved link would a target-dependent perturbation contrast address, and which would remain genetic?

<details data-worked-answer>
<summary>Check the reasoning after your attempt</summary>

An alternative effector U and a detection or viability effect remain possible. A well-defined target-dependent contrast could address the T-to-phenotype link in that context, while the variant-to-effector link needs its own evidence. Continue to [genetic target hypotheses](/learn/mechanism-targets/genetics-to-target-evidence/) and [perturbation validation](/learn/mechanism-targets/perturbation-to-target-validation/) for separate assumptions and worked cases.

## Continue with genetic and perturbation evidence

Start by writing down the disease state a hypothesis is about. [Define the disease
state before the target](/learn/mechanism-targets/disease-state-definition/) works
one randomised trial through six fields. The [genetic-evidence lesson](/learn/mechanism-targets/genetics-to-target-evidence/)
works through fine mapping, colocalization and instrumental-variable assumptions.
Then compare [perturbation across contexts](/learn/mechanism-targets/perturbation-context-generalization/).
Neither association nor transferred prediction is a complete causal target validation.

## Core-source path

Trace a pathway in [Reactome](https://reactome.org/), inspect interactions in
[BioGRID](https://thebiogrid.org/), and use the target-to-candidate lesson's
explicit failure gates.
