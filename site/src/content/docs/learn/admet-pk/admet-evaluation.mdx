## Foundations

ADMET covers absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, and toxicity;
PK/PD adds exposure-response relationships. These are distinct endpoints with
different experimental systems and uncertainty. A predicted toxicity probability
is not a safety conclusion.

## Practical workflow

For each proposed molecule, list the decision, relevant assay, species and
matrix, uncertainty, and the next experiment. Use
[ADMET-AI](https://github.com/swansonk14/admet_ai) or a simple descriptor model
as a reproducible comparison only after checking the task source and splits.

## Evaluation

Audit label definitions, assay censoring, chemical series leakage, and the
translation gap from in vitro to in vivo exposure. Evaluate calibration and
decision thresholds on validation data, not after candidate selection.

## Frontier and open questions

Physical-property tools such as the
[OpenFF Evaluator](https://github.com/openforcefield/openff-evaluator) may
constrain a hypothesis, but they do not replace metabolism, transporter, or
toxicology experiments.

## A bounded worked decision and independent task

Read [assay controls](/learn/drug-discovery-foundations/assay-controls-dose-response/) first. The task is to identify an ADMET endpoint before comparing predictions. In a constructed record pair, one value is intrinsic clearance per million hepatocytes and the other is clearance per gram of microsomal protein. The species name can match while the measurement basis differs. A numerical ratio between those raw values does not have a validated physiological meaning.

An independent reviewer asks for a single clearance ranking across the pair. Write the fields that must be retained and explain the first obstacle to that ranking.

<details data-worked-answer>

Retain species, matrix, endpoint, units, parent identity, conditions and the source. The immediate obstacle is the incompatible measurement basis, before model choice or rank correlation. A defensible comparison needs a specified, validated bridge or separate endpoint-specific analysis. Continue to [exposure and PK/PD](/learn/admet-pk/exposure-clearance-pkpd/) for the derived-quantity boundary.
