## Foundations

Docking, scoring, molecular dynamics, free-energy calculations, and quantum
chemistry trade different approximations for different decisions. A docking
pose is not a free-energy estimate, and a relative free-energy calculation is
not a proof of experimental potency.

## Practical workflow

Write the decision, protein construct and state, ligand series, protonation
assumptions, force field, solvent model, and experimental comparator before a
calculation. Use a cheap baseline first, then increase protocol cost only when
it changes a decision. The [Open Force Field Toolkit](https://docs.openforcefield.org/projects/toolkit/en/stable/)
and [OpenMM](https://openmm.org/) document distinct pieces of such workflows.

## Evaluation

Evaluate against an experimental endpoint matched to the protocol. Report
failed transformations, uncertainty, convergence diagnostics, and the
independent chemical series or target state used for generalization. Do not
compare methods whose inputs or thermodynamic endpoints differ.

## Frontier and open questions

[OpenFE](https://github.com/OpenFreeEnergy/openfe),
[Perses](https://github.com/choderalab/perses), and
[GROMACS](https://www.gromacs.org/) make different workflow choices. The open
question is whether the available protocol, compute budget, and assay noise
support the decision at hand, not which brand has a universal advantage.

## Check whether two scores may be compared

The [docking protocol lab](/labs/docking-protocol/) separates the seven components
a score comes out of and works out which reported numbers are on one scale. Two
protocols differing in a single component report -8.2 and -10.7 for the same
ligand on the same structure, no pair supports a controlled ligand ranking under its declared rule, and a protocol
declaring no validation still produces a full column of numbers. Running
`python labs/docking_protocol/inspect_protocol.py empirical-docking` extends this
to `3` real RCSB PDB EGFR co-crystals (`1M17`, `4HJO`, `1XKK`, `157` pocket heavy
atoms within `10` Å of `AQ4`): across `28` poses, the crystal pose (`0.0` Å RMSD,
Met793 N distance `2.6991` Å, `0` clashes `< 2.4` Å) shifts from `-15.6194` (vdW +
H-bond) to `-12.46` (vdW-only rescoring, gap `+3.1594`), the top redocked pose
(`0.3002` Å RMSD, Met793 N distance `2.6624` Å) scores `-15.961` (`-12.88`
vdW-only), cross-docking `4HJO` (`+11.8116`, `5` clashes, `1.8836` Å) or DFG-out
`1XKK` (`+261.2519`, `31` clashes, `4.1362` Å) into rigid `1M17` exposes receptor-state
incompatibility, and decoy enrichment across `16` candidates (`4` active poses vs
`12` decoys) reaches `ROC-AUC = 1.0` and `EF_25% = 4.0`.
