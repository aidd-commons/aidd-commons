## Foundations

A discovery program connects a disease-relevant mechanism, a tractable target,
a chemical or biologic intervention, and an experiment that can falsify the
proposed mechanism. [ChEMBL](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/),
[PubChem](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/), and
[DrugBank](https://go.drugbank.com/) are useful identity and annotation sources;
their public pages do not automatically grant bulk-data or redistribution rights.

## Practical workflow

Define the decision before collecting features: target nomination, hit triage,
or lead optimization each needs a different unit and assay context. Normalize
chemical identity, assay endpoint, organism, target construct, and measurement
conditions. Use a simple [RDKit](https://github.com/rdkit/rdkit) descriptor or
substructure check before relying on a learned representation.

## Evaluation

Check whether a reported activity is comparable across assay formats, and
whether a historical analogue leaked across the split. A docking score or a
single biochemical assay is not evidence of cellular efficacy, selectivity, or
developability.

## Frontier and open questions

Multimodal target-to-candidate systems may join chemical, structural, and
mechanistic evidence. Their utility depends on whether the linkages and the
downstream experimental feedback are available at the same decision point.

## End-to-end therapeutic decision context over real EGFR NSCLC and ChEMBL 37 records

Before running a molecular representation or generative model, a discovery programme must specify the full decision chain connecting: (1) clinical disease need, (2) biological intervention hypothesis, (3) therapeutic modality choice, (4) target-product-profile (TPP) gate thresholds, and (5) the next falsifying experiment. Run the fresh independent audit in [the target-product-profile laboratory](/labs/target-product-profile/):

```bash
.venv/bin/python labs/target_product_profile/inspect_target_product_profile.py decision-context
.venv/bin/python labs/target_product_profile/inspect_target_product_profile.py check-decision-context
```

1. **Clinical need and disease state (`MONDO_0005233`)**: In EGFR-mutant (`exon 19` deletion / `L858R`) advanced non-small-cell lung cancer with central nervous system (CNS) metastases, Open Targets 26.03 records an overall association score of `0.8503` (`somatic_mutation = 0.8316`), `21` curated wild-type EGFR safety events (rash, diarrhea, interstitial lung disease), and the Phase III IPASS subgroup reversal (`EGFR`-mutant PFS `HR = 0.48` vs wild-type `HR = 2.85`, ratio `5.94`). First-generation reversible quinazolines fail via acquired `Thr790Met` (`T790M`) gatekeeper resistance (`3.14`–`3.48` Å contact in PDB `1M17`/`4HJO`/`1XKK`) and incomplete CNS penetration.
2. **4-modality comparison**:
   - **Oral brain-penetrant small-molecule kinase inhibitor** (`selected_primary_modality`): reaches the intracellular kinase domain, crosses the intact blood-brain barrier for CNS metastases, supports chronic oral dosing, and discriminates the mutant ATP/allosteric pocket; primary risks are `CYP3A4` drug-drug interaction (`pChEMBL <= 5.0` gate), hepatic microsome clearance (`<= 50 mL.min-1.g-1` gate), and wild-type epithelial EGFR toxicity.
   - **Extracellular anti-EGFR IgG1 monoclonal antibody** (`rejected_for_intracellular_cns_indication`): a `150` kDa IgG1 has `< 0.1%` CSF-to-plasma penetration across an intact blood-brain barrier and binds the extracellular domain (`1IVO`/`1YY9`) without discriminating intracellular `L858R`/`T790M` from wild-type EGFR.
   - **Heterobifunctional PROTAC degrader** (`backup_for_catalytic_independent_scaffolding_resistance`): reaches the intracellular kinase and ablates scaffolding function, but high molecular weight (`850`–`1,100` Da) and polar surface area (`> 160` Å²) limit oral bioavailability and intact-BBB CNS exposure.
   - **Lipid-nanoparticle (LNP) siRNA** (`rejected_due_to_extrahepatic_tumour_delivery_bottleneck`): systemic ionizable LNPs distribute predominantly to liver hepatocytes via ApoE rather than extrahepatic lung and CNS tumours.
3. **ChEMBL 37 TPP funnel (`7,908` -> `58` -> `37` -> `0`)**: Across the `7,908`-parent ChEMBL 37 union (`7,850` single-family parents excluded), `58` parents have at least `2` measured endpoint families. Applying the frozen mandatory gates (`response_ic50 <= 1000 nM`, human liver microsome clearance `<= 50 mL.min-1.g-1`, `CYP3A4 <= 5.0 pChEMBL`, clinical response `>= 30%` ORR) contradicts `21` parents (`20` fail `CYP3A4`, `5` fail human microsome clearance, `1` fails response `IC50`), leaves `37` parents with no observed gate failure, and yields `0` profile-satisfying compounds because `57` of `58` lack an admitted `response_ic50` value and all `58` lack clinical response and immunogenicity (`<= 5%` ADA) measurements (`12` miss rat hepatocyte clearance `<= 30 uL.min-1.(10^6cells)-1` and `19` miss `CYP2D6 <= 5.0 pChEMBL`).
4. **Next falsifiable experimental decisions**: For the `37` uncontradicted parents, Step `1` measures paired cellular phospho-EGFR `IC50` in mutant (`H1975` `L858R`/`T790M`) vs wild-type (`A431`) cells, rejecting any parent with mutant `IC50 > 100 nM` or wild-type/mutant selectivity ratio `< 10`x. Step `2` runs bidirectional `MDCK-MDR1` and `MDCK-BCRP` permeability and efflux assays, rejecting any compound with `Papp < 5e-6 cm/s` or efflux ratio `ER > 2.0` before in vivo `Kp,uu,brain` studies.

## A bounded worked decision and independent task

Begin with [the pipeline](/learn/foundations/getting-started/). This orientation asks what evidence belongs to a discovery decision. Consider two problems: (A) a constructed molecule that lowers a reporter signal and also acts in a direct reporter counterscreen, and (B) choosing between an extracellular anti-EGFR IgG1 antibody and an oral brain-penetrant small-molecule kinase inhibitor for `L858R`/`T790M` NSCLC with brain metastases, where `37` of `58` ChEMBL 37 multi-family parents have no observed gate failure.

1. For (A), write the next orthogonal assay question without calling the molecule an effective drug.
2. For (B), state why the IgG1 antibody is rejected for this indication, why the `37` uncontradicted ChEMBL parents still have `0` profile-satisfying compounds, and which two quantitative assays falsify the remaining candidates next.

1. A bounded question asks whether the response persists when the detection method changes while the relevant biological target and cellular context are retained. That tests detection interference rather than clinical efficacy.

An ~`150` kDa IgG1 antibody achieves `< 0.1%` CSF-to-plasma exposure across an intact blood-brain barrier and binds the ectodomain without discriminating intracellular `L858R`/`T790M` from wild-type EGFR (`21` curated safety liabilities). The `37` ChEMBL parents with no observed gate failure yield `0` profile-satisfying compounds because `57` of `58` cohort parents are unmeasured on `response_ic50` and all `58` are unmeasured on clinical response (`>= 30%` ORR).
